# Arise — Genre Atlas: MC Systems Across Anime, Manga & Manhwa

> The user's question: *what do the systems and protagonists of this genre actually
> teach, and what does Arise take from each?* This is the borrow/refuse ledger.
>
> Companions: `SYSTEM-RESEARCH.md` (Solo Leveling mechanics in detail) and
> `SYSTEM-GENRE-DEEP.md` (the game-design lineage). This doc widens the lens from
> one work to the whole genre, and ends with what we deliberately do **not** take.

---

## 1. The grammar every "System" story uses

The genre is compositional, not formulaic. Community analysis converges on a
**two-axis grammar**:

| Axis | Options | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| **Premise trope** | regression · possession · returner · reincarnation · awakened-late · chosen-one | *why* the hero is different |
| **Mechanic** | status window / system · constellations / sponsors · class & skills · gates & dungeons | *how* growth is made visible |
| **Progression axis** | level · rank E→S · stat points · skill tiers · titles | the shape of the curve |
| **Emotional engine** | unfairness → vindication · competence → respect · service → belonging | why the reader keeps reading |

Any single work picks one option per column. That is why "a regressor in a murim
sect who also has a status window" feels natural rather than crowded.

**For Arise:** we take the **mechanic** (status window, classes, skills, ranks)
and the **emotional engine** (unfairness → vindication), and we take **no premise
trope at all** — because in real life the only premise is "you, now". That is not
a gap in the fantasy; it is the point.

## 2. The mechanic catalogue — what each one is actually for

| Mechanic | Narrative job | Disciplined use in Arise |
|---|---|---|
| **Status window** | makes the invisible visible; the reader's dashboard | **Yes** — the core screen. Effort rendered legible. |
| **Quests** | turns intention into a call to action; the cliffhanger machine | **Yes** — but user-authored or science-derived, never manufactured urgency |
| **Penalties** | raises stakes, creates dread | **Narrow** — only self-chosen, reversible, never shame |
| **Ranks E→S** | long-horizon identity; a story you can point at | **Yes** — the spine of v1 |
| **Stat points** | meaningful trade-offs | **Later** (v2) — real self-assessment, not fake RPG numbers |
| **Classes** | identity and specialisation | **Later** (v2) — "Job" as a self-chosen archetype |
| **Skill trees** | visible growth | **Later** — mapped to real competencies |
| **Titles** | recognition of a milestone | **Later** (v6) — earned, never purchasable |
| **Bestiary** | names the enemy | **Later** (v6) — the "monster" is a named obstacle pattern |
| **Gacha / loot boxes** | variable-ratio dopamine | **Never** — red line (slot-machine rewards) |
| **Shop with real money** | monetisation | **Never** — red line (real-money stakes) |
| **Leaderboards / guilds** | social comparison | **Never** — personal & private, and social proof is a red line |

> The genre's most addictive mechanic — variable-ratio reward — is the one we
> cannot have. That is a **design constraint, not a limitation**: it forces the
> loop to be honest, and honest loops are what make a discipline tool survive past
> week three.

## 3. The archetype atlas

Grouped by what they teach a *discipline* app, not by popularity.

### 3.1 The System protagonist (the direct lineage)

| Work | Medium | Premise | What it teaches | Arise takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo Leveling** | manhwa/anime | awakened-late, system | a status window turns invisible effort into visible growth; legible fairness is the whole fantasy | the System aesthetic + E→S ranks + the daily-quest loop |
| **The Gamer** | manhwa | system | stats/skills as an interface layer over ordinary life | the idea that *any* real skill can be a skill tree |
| **Tower of God** | manhwa/anime | chosen-one, trials | progression as a climb with tests; allies matter | floor/level structure; companions later (v11) — never a social feed |
| **Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint** | manhwa/novel | reader-insert, scenarios | knowledge of the "script" is power; survival by preparation | the **daily log** as the reader's own record |
| **SSS-Class Suicide Hunter** | manhwa/novel | regression, system | failure is survivable; repetition is the teacher | **never-miss-twice** framing; failure has a path back |
| **Nano Machine** | manhwa | possession, murim | the system as an *external discipline* imposed on a lazy body | the System as a coach, not a judge |
| **Return of the Mount Hua Sect** | manhwa | regression, murim | returning to a place you failed; rebuilding from nothing | "context reset" — re-anchoring after a relapse or move |
| **Leveling Up with the Gods** | manhwa | system, regression | stat allocation as identity choice | v2 stat points, honestly self-assessed |
| **The Beginning After the End** | novel/manhwa | reincarnation | a second chance used *well* — effort over talent | long-horizon identity; rank as story not score |
| **Overgeared / The Legendary Moonlight Sculptor** | novel/manhwa | VR game | persistence in a grindy system eventually compounds | the grind is the point; celebrate consistency, not spikes |

### 3.2 The non-System discipline protagonists (the deeper lesson)

These matter more than the System stories, because they show discipline *without*
a stat window — which is the real problem Arise solves.

| Character | Work | What they embody | Arise takes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rock Lee** | Naruto | zero talent, maximal effort; hard work against genius | the **Effort** pillar; ranks earned, never gifted |
| **Saitama** | One Punch Man | a boring daily routine that actually worked; the anticlimax of mastery | routine is unglamorous by design; don't sell spectacle |
| **Ippo Makunouchi** | Hajime no Ippo | incremental training, coachability, repeated basics | skill trees built from fundamentals |
| **Isagi / Rin / Bachira** | Blue Lock | ego as fuel; competition with *yourself* | ambition without shame; the rival is your last self |
| **Thorfinn** | Vinland Saga | from revenge to purpose; identity rebuilt after violence | "the real enemy" is the old self — v8 Awakening |
| **Mob** | Mob Psycho 100 | growth through honesty and ordinary kindness, not power | the System must never reward cruelty or dominance |
| **Frieren** | Frieren | patience across a very long horizon; small rituals accumulate | long arcs; the v14 Life Chapters frame |
| **Vegeta** | Dragon Ball | pride as a driver, and its cost | ambition is allowed; arrogance is not rewarded |

### 3.3 What the antagonists teach

The genre's best villains are **internal patterns externalised**: the monarch who
possesses you, the regression that tempts you to undo, the system that owns you.

- **Solo Leveling**: the System has an author — power with an agenda.
- **ORV**: the story consumes its reader.
- **Vinland Saga**: revenge is a habit.

> **Arise takes:** the final boss is **the old self**, and the System is explicitly
> *yours* ("The System belongs to you"). The app never pretends to be an external
> authority with power over the user — that is the genre's darkest implication and
> we invert it.

## 4. What "levels" and "ranks" mean when they are real

The genre's progression is **monotonic** (never goes down) and **public** (others
see it). Arise must be neither:

| Genre convention | Reality | Arise's answer |
|---|---|---|
| Levels never drop | progress is genuinely non-linear | **XP ledger is monotonic**; rank can be re-earned, never publicly lost |
| Ranks are public status | this is a private app | rank is **private**; only the user sees it |
| Quests are issued to you | autonomy is the active ingredient (SDT) | quests are **user-authored** or **science-derived**, always editable |
| Penalties punish | shame kills adherence | penalties are **self-chosen and reversible** |
| The System is external | ownership predicts persistence | the System is **yours**; you can rewrite its text |

## 5. The borrow/refuse ledger (binding)

**Borrow:** status window · quest loop · E→S ranks · classes/skills (v2+) · titles
(v6) · bestiary-as-obstacle-pattern (v6) · dungeons-as-challenge-blocks (v4) ·
shadow army as *accumulated evidence of past effort* (v10) · the emotional engine
of unfairness→vindication.

**Refuse** (cross-checked against the 12 red lines in `PSYCHOLOGY.md`): gacha and
variable rewards · real-money stakes · public leaderboards · guilds and social
comparison · streak shame · manufactured urgency · pay-to-win · "the System owns
you" framing · punitive HP that removes progress · any mechanic whose only hook is
anxiety.

**IP boundary:** no Solo Leveling names, terms, rank wording, or iconography.
Borrow the *grammar*, never the *text* (locked decision 5 in `AGENTS.md`).
